May 2026, written just for you

Dearest Adhya,

I don't know if anyone has ever told you this properly, so let me be the one to say it — you are genuinely, unreasonably, wonderfully cute.

Not in a passing way. Not just your face, though yes, absolutely your face. But the whole of you — the way you carry yourself, the way you laugh like you mean it, the way your eyes do that thing where they say everything before your mouth does.

There are people who light up a room when they walk in. You are one of those people. You probably don't notice it, which honestly makes it even more true.

Someone looked at you today — really looked — and thought: "I am so glad she exists."

That someone is me.

So here it is, officially, in print: Adhya — you are absolutely precious, and the world is a better, warmer, softer place because you are in it.

Yours, always — someone who means every word
