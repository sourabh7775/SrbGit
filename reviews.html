3 June 2026 · Two month update · Final review (probably)
Two months.

I have now written four reviews of this restaurant. I am aware this is unusual behaviour for a dining establishment.

Here is what I know two months later: the food was not the point. The staircase was the point. She was the point.

Adhya came up those stairs and I was already sitting there and something just — shifted. And it has not shifted back. And I do not want it to.

I think about her when I wake up. I think about her at 10:47 during work. I think about her at 23:59 before I sleep. She is the best thing that has happened to me and she arrived via a staircase in a restaurant I came to for a family lunch.

Life is very strange and also very good.
Happy 2 months, Adhya. 🤍
from the guy who was already sitting there
